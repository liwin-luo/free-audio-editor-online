How to Trim Audio Online with Loop Preview

Step 1: Upload Your Audio
Click "Upload Your Audio File" and select the MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, or OGG file you want to trim. The file loads instantly in your browser with a visual waveform.

Step 2: Select Your Cut
Drag the blue handles on the waveform to select the section you want to keep. The time displays update in real-time showing start time, end time, and duration of your selection.

Step 3: Preview Your Selection (New!)
Press the spacebar or click the "Preview Selection" button to hear your cut instantly before downloading. Enable loop to continuously hear your selection while adjusting handles. Change playback speed to 0.5x for precise editing or 2x for quick scanning. This is perfect for finding exact guitar riffs, vocal hooks, or beat drops for TikTok and Instagram Reels.

Step 4: Fine-Tune with Real-Time Feedback
While preview is playing (especially with loop enabled), drag the handles to adjust your selection. The green progress line shows exactly where you are in the audio. Keep adjusting until it's perfect - no more guessing!

Step 5: Add Fade Effects (Optional)
Check "Add Fade In" to smoothly fade in at the beginning, or "Add Fade Out" to gradually fade out at the end. This creates professional-sounding transitions and is perfect for ringtones or music clips.

Step 6: Cut and Download
Once your preview sounds perfect, click "Cut Audio" and wait a few seconds. Your trimmed audio file will download automatically, ready to use for ringtones, TikTok videos, Instagram Reels, YouTube intros, or whatever you need.
