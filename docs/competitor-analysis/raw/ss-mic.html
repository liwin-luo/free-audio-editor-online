    What the mic test checks

    CheckWhat it meansWhat hurts it
    ClarityHow much of your voice's high detail comes through: the "s", "t" and "f" sounds speech recognition needs.Bluetooth headsets in call mode (they stop near 4 kHz), cheap or covered mics.
    Background noiseHow far your voice stands above the room.Fans, traffic, a laptop's own fan, a mic far from your mouth.
    EchoHow cleanly each word stops. A mic near your mouth hears your voice; one at arm's length also hears the room bouncing it back.Bare rooms, a laptop mic at arm's length.
    VolumeWhether the input is loud enough.A mic turned down in your sound settings, or pointing away from you.
    DistortionCrackle on loud words, or a mains hum under the voice.Input set too high, a ground loop on a USB or headphone-jack mic.

    Why do AirPods and Bluetooth headsets sound bad on a computer?

    The moment a website or app uses a Bluetooth headset's microphone, the headset switches from its music mode to its call mode. Call mode sends narrow audio that stops at about 4 kHz, which is why your voice sounds muffled, like a phone call, and why transcripts made from it miss words. The mic test spots this and says so. For recording, a wired headset or your computer's built-in mic usually sounds much better.

    Which microphone is best for recording?

    The one closest to your mouth that is not in call mode, in the quietest room you have. Every microphone hears the same sentence at the same moment, so the scores compare like for like. Press Record with it to go straight to the free voice recorder with that microphone chosen.

    Why does my microphone not work in the browser?▼
    Usually the browser is blocking the microphone for the site, another app is already using it, or no microphone is plugged in. The test tells you which one it is and shows the steps for your browser.
    What is a good mic test score?▼
    80 or more is a clear, close microphone that is good for recording and transcription. 55 to 80 works but has a weakness the test names, such as echo or noise. Under 55 usually means a Bluetooth headset in call mode, a mic far from your mouth, or a noisy room.
