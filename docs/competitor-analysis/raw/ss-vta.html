How It Works

The tool uses FFmpeg.wasm — the full FFmpeg multimedia toolkit compiled to WebAssembly — to demux your video and re-encode the audio stream into your chosen format. Because FFmpeg.wasm runs entirely in your browser, your video never touches a server and there's no upload service in the middle.
