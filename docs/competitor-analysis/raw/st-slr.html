What is Slowed and Reverb Audio?

Slowed and reverb is a popular audio effect that reduces the tempo of a song while adding an echo-like reverb effect. This creates a dreamy, atmospheric sound that's become hugely popular on TikTok, YouTube, and social media for music edits, aesthetic videos, and creating chill vibes.

Looking to create slowed reverb audio specifically for TikTok videos? Try our TikTok Slowed Reverb Maker — it's optimized for TikTok-ready audio output.

Popular Slowed Audio Presets

TikTok Slowed (80% speed, medium reverb): The most popular preset for TikTok edits and social media videos. Creates that signature slowed and reverb sound that's trending everywhere.

Nightcore Reverse (75% speed, light reverb): The opposite of nightcore - instead of speeding up, we slow down. Perfect for creating dramatic, emotional versions of songs with subtle atmosphere.

Vaporwave Aesthetic (65% speed, heavy reverb): Deep, dreamy, nostalgic vibes. This extreme slowed and reverb setting creates that classic vaporwave sound perfect for aesthetic videos and chill playlists.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make slowed and reverb audio?

Upload your audio file, choose a preset like 'TikTok Slowed' (80% speed, medium reverb) or adjust the speed and reverb sliders manually for custom settings, then click 'Create Slowed Audio' to download your track with the slowed tempo and reverb effect applied.

What audio formats are supported?

Our slowed and reverb maker supports MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, and OGG audio formats. Upload any of these formats and download your slowed audio as high-quality MP3.

Is this slowed and reverb maker free?

Yes, completely free with no file limits, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges. Create unlimited slowed and reverb audio tracks for TikTok, YouTube, or personal use.

How long does it take to create slowed audio?

Most audio files process in under 30 seconds. The exact time depends on your file size and the speed/reverb settings you choose, but even large files typically complete within a minute.

What is the TikTok Slowed preset?

TikTok Slowed preset sets your audio to 80% of the original speed with medium reverb effect, creating the popular slowed and reverb sound commonly used in TikTok videos, Instagram reels, and social media edits.

Can I slow down a song without changing the pitch?

Yes! Our tool slows down the audio while preserving the original pitch, so the song doesn't sound lower or "chipmunk-like" - just slower with reverb. This maintains the natural sound of the vocals and instruments.

What's the difference between the presets?

TikTok Slowed (80% speed, medium reverb) is the most popular for social media. Nightcore Reverse (75% speed, light reverb) is more dramatic with subtle atmosphere. Vaporwave Aesthetic (65% speed, heavy reverb) creates deep, dreamy vibes for extreme slow-down effects.

Can I use slowed and reverb audio on TikTok and YouTube?

Yes, you can use your created slowed and reverb audio in your own videos. However, remember that the original song may still be subject to copyright. Use royalty-free music or music you have rights to for the best legal protection.

Do I need to install any software?

No installation required! This slowed and reverb maker works entirely in your web browser. Just visit this page, upload your audio, adjust settings, and download your slowed track.

Can I adjust the speed and reverb separately?

Yes, absolutely. Choose the 'Custom' preset and use the two sliders to independently control speed (50-100%) and reverb amount (0-200%). This gives you complete creative control over your slowed audio sound, including extreme reverb effects for experimental and ambient sounds.

Will slowed audio sound good?

Yes. The pitch is preserved when the track is slowed, so vocals and instruments keep their natural character rather than dropping into a chipmunk or demon-voice effect, and the reverb tail is applied smoothly. Output is a high-quality MP3. Bear in mind that slowing audio down is a real transformation of the original recording — at 65% and below the effect is meant to be obvious.

Can I slow down audio more than 50%?

Currently, the tool supports slowing down to 50% of original speed (half speed). This covers most use cases from subtle slowing to extreme vaporwave effects. For most slowed and reverb tracks, 65-80% speed is ideal.
