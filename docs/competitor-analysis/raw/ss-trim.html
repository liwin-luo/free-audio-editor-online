Trim & Cut Audio Online — Free, Private, No Sign-up

SnipSound's audio trimmer is a full waveform editor that runs entirely in your web browser. Drop in an MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AAC or M4A file, split it at any point, delete the segments you don't want, and download the result. Your files never leave your device.

What you can do

  • Trim the start and end — chop off silence, intros, or ad reads.
  • Cut a section out of the middle — split with C, delete the unwanted segment, the rest stitches back together with a short crossfade so seams are inaudible.
  • Make multiple cuts — split as many times as you want; remove every segment you don't need.
  • Combine multiple files — drop additional clips in and concatenate them with optional crossfade.
  • Export to any format — MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or AAC.
  • Cut MP3 without a re-encode of the parts you keep — the segments you keep are joined and exported once, so you are not stacking lossy passes.

Why trim audio in the browser

  • 100% client-side. Other trimmers upload your files to a server. We don't — the audio never leaves your device.
  • No cap imposed by us. Large files stream in pieces rather than loading, so memory is not the ceiling it is in most browser tools.
  • No sign-up, no ads, no watermarks.
  • Lossless waveform editing. Decoding happens once; export re-encodes only the segments you keep.

Audio trimmer FAQ

How do I trim an audio file online?▼
Drop your audio file onto the page (or click to browse). Use the playhead and press C at the split point, then Delete any segment you don't want. Pick an output format and click Export. The trimmed file downloads to your device.
Can I cut multiple sections out of a single file?▼
Yes. Split as many times as you need with C, then delete every segment you want gone. The remaining segments are stitched back together on export with a short crossfade so cuts are inaudible.
What audio formats can I trim?▼
Upload MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AAC or M4A. Export to MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or AAC.
Is my audio uploaded to a server?▼
No. SnipSound's trimmer runs entirely in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your files never leave your device.
Is there a file size limit?▼
No hard limit — the only constraint is your device's available memory. Most trims of a song-length file complete in seconds.
Will my cuts sound clean or will I hear a click?▼
Clean. The trimmer applies a short crossfade at each seam so the audio glides through cuts without the pop you sometimes get from a hard splice.
